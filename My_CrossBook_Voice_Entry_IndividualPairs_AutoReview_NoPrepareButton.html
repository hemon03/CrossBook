Quick Buy — House / Ending
House adds the amount to 10 consecutive numbers (00–09, 10–19, …, 90–99). Ending adds the amount to 10 numbers (e.g. ending 7: 07, 17, …, 97). Pair adds the amount to one double number (00, 11, 22, …, 99), or select ALL PAIRS to add it to all 10 double numbers at once. Amounts are added to the existing totals.
Tap denominations to add stake. House and Ending tools apply the entered amount to each matching number. Save PDF before starting a new day.
